Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how 0618soft handles personal information in connection with 0618soft.com and the mobile applications we publish (together, the “Services”). 0618soft is an independent software developer. To ask a question or make a privacy request, email 0618soft@gmail.com.

Information we collect

What we receive depends on how you use the Services.

  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address, the content of your message, and any information you choose to include.
  • App and device data. When you use an app, we may process information needed to run it, such as device type, operating system version, language, app version, and diagnostic information if the app reports an error. A particular app will ask for access, such as photos or contacts, only when a feature needs it, and your device settings control that access.
  • Purchases. If you buy a feature or subscription through the Apple App Store or Google Play, the store processes the payment. We may learn that a purchase or subscription is active so the app can unlock what you bought. We do not receive or store your full payment card number.
  • The website. This website is a static site. It does not have user accounts. It does not set tracking cookies, and it does not use advertising or analytics tools. The host that delivers the site may still process standard technical data, such as an IP address, in its own server logs.

How we use information

We use personal information to reply to you, operate and maintain the apps, provide purchases you request, diagnose problems, protect the security of the Services, and comply with law.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only in these cases:

  • with Apple or Google when you download an app or make a purchase through their stores, under those stores’ own terms and privacy policies;
  • with a service provider that processes information only to host or operate a Service for us, and only under instructions consistent with this policy;
  • when disclosure is required by law, legal process, or a government request, or when it is needed to protect the rights, safety, and security of 0618soft, our users, or others.

Cookies

The website does not set tracking cookies and does not use advertising or analytics cookies. An app may store information locally on your device so that it can function. You can review and change app permissions in your device settings.

Retention

We keep email correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and for a reasonable period afterward. Purchase status is kept while an entitlement is active and for as long as we need it for records and support. Diagnostic information is kept only for as long as it is useful for understanding and fixing problems. We delete or de-identify information when we no longer need it, unless the law requires a longer period.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to an independent developer and to the kind of information involved. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, email us and we will delete it.

United States and California residents

We handle the Services under United States law. If you use the Services from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States and in other places where our hosting providers operate.

If you are a California resident, you may ask us to disclose the categories of personal information we have collected about you, and you may ask us to delete that information, subject to exceptions allowed by law. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those ideas are used in the California Consumer Privacy Act. To make a request, email 0618soft@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. We may need to verify that the request comes from you.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do. If a change is material, we will post the updated policy here before it applies.
